Sarv OTP

Two-factor codes that never leave your phone.

A free, open-source authenticator for Android and iOS. Scan the QR code a service shows you and Sarv OTP generates the rolling 6-digit codes — fully offline, with secrets locked in your device's keystore. Works anywhere "Authenticator app" 2FA is offered: Google, GitHub, AWS and more.

Why Sarv OTP

Offline by design

No account, no sync, no network calls. Codes are computed from the secret and the clock — it works in airplane mode.

Keystore-backed

Secrets live in Android Keystore / iOS Keychain, with optional biometric app lock and screenshot blocking.

Standards-based

Implements TOTP (RFC 6238), HOTP (RFC 4226) and otpauth:// — the same standards Google Authenticator uses.

Encrypted backup

Export your accounts to a password-protected file and import them on a new phone.
